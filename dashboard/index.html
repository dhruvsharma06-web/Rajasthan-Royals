How it works

  1. Pressure is stakes

    A win-probability model, logistic regression on Duckworth-Lewis-style features fitted leaving each season out, gives every ball its stakes: the expected swing in win probability over the usual mix of outcomes. The top 20% within format and innings are high pressure.

  2. Credit against the same situation

    Each ball's change in win probability is credited to batter and bowler, then compared with the average player in the same format, season, innings, phase and wickets-down band. Run-outs charge the dismissed batter, never the bowler.

  3. Rank cautiously

    Pressure balls measure pressure performance directly; ordinary balls help only as far as clutch differences cannot distort them. Estimates are shrunk toward the average and ranked on the 20th percentile of 1,000 match bootstraps.
